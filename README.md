# About Us

The Centre of Excellence in Artificial Intelligence for Digital Humanities (CoE AI4DH) is a community 
of experts in AI, social sciences, and digital humanities who pursue excellent science in an 
interdisciplinary and collaborative way.

By leveraging AI expertise and a top-tier computational infrastructure, the CoE provides humanities 
researchers with novel methods and workflows that can enhance the efficiency and depth of their analyses, 
leading to new insights and a broader understanding of cultural and historical phenomena.

Our primary goal is to strengthen collaboration across disciplines, including computer science, AI, 
linguistics, history, media studies, folkloristics, journalism, and philosophy.

# Research Focus

Our research currently focuses on the following topics, among others:
- Natural Language Processing for historical newspaper and media analysis
- Tools for less-resourced languages
- Production of semantic data and evaluation datasets from lexical resources
- Knowledge injection into Large Language Models
- AI infrastructure for data-driven folkloristics
- Explanation of generative large language models
